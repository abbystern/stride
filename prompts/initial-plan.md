# Prompt Log: Initial Project Plan

## Feature
Initial planning for Stride, an all-in-one training, nutrition, and recovery application.

## Key AI Prompts

**Prompt 1: App concept**

"I'm thinking of making some kind of health tracker, marathon training app, or running log."

**Prompt 2: Defining the user need**

"I want a place to store my workouts, my run plan, and my nutrition, as well as how I'm feeling and recovering. I want to track progressive overload, monitor whether I'm properly fueling, balance training load with recovery, and plan workouts around my schedule."

**Prompt 3: Running planner**

"I use Garmin Run Coach, but I need more flexibility. I want suggestions for distance, pace, time on feet, and elevation, with the ability to move workouts between days and change my weekly plan."

**Prompt 4: Strength training**

"I want to look at my lifting plan while I'm doing a workout and add my weights as I go so that I have something to follow along with."

**Prompt 5: Project planning**

"Create a complete PLAN.md for Stride that meets the assignment requirements, including user stories, database tables and relationships, tests, technologies, design, risks, and future Garmin/Oura integrations."

## AI Agent Output and Decisions

The AI helped translate my initial idea into a structured project plan with seven required features and three custom features.

I made several product decisions during planning:

- Prioritized a flexible weekly planner over a rigid marathon calendar.
- Required live set-by-set strength logging rather than recording weights only after workouts.
- Chose Oura as the future source for daily recovery data and Garmin for completed running activities.
- Deferred wearable integrations until after the assignment to keep the initial application achievable.
- Decided that users will create their own workout templates rather than hardcoding my personal lifting routines.

## Outcome

Created `PLAN.md` as the project's original blueprint before beginning feature development.
